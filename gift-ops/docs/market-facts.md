## Population & households
- US Muslim population **4.5–5M** (US Religion Census 2020; Pew Research estimates).
- **~1.5–2M Muslim households** — derived from population ÷ average household size.

## Spend
- **$170.8B** annual US Muslim consumer spend (DinarStandard, Thrive 2025 report).
- **Income barbell** (ISPU 2025): **35%** of Muslim households earn under $30K, **24%** earn over $100K. Pricing targets the premium third — the ladder's Mid and Premium tiers exist for the 24%.
- Muslim adults skew young: **26%** are aged 18–24 vs **10%** of the general public — digital-native gift buyers, mobile-first shopping.

## Category
- Islamic wall décor: **$2.8B** market, **7.6% CAGR**; **customization is the top trend** — personalization is the category's growth engine, not a niche.

## Calendar (2027 season)

- **Eid al-Adha ≈ mid-May 2027** — second gifting season.
- **35–40% of season spend lands at Eid** — shipping cutoffs are the campaign (knowledge/eid-ramadan-campaign.md).
