# Adab Guardrails

Non-negotiable. These rules override any sales, SEO, or margin consideration. They may not be altered without an explicit operator decision, and even then only toward greater strictness.

## The rule
Ayat of the Qur'an and the Names of Allah are **never** placed on:
- floor items (rugs, mats, doormats),
- cutting boards,
- coasters,
- any item routinely soiled or discarded (napkins, wrapping, disposable packaging, pet items, footwear).

**Utility items** (boards, coasters, trays, boxes, kitchenware) carry **names, duas, and geometry only**.

**Ayat are reserved for wall pieces** — items hung or displayed with respect, out of the path of soiling.

## On every listing
This policy is stated verbatim in every listing description (see /listing):

> "Out of respect (adab), we never place Qur'anic verses or the Names of Allah on cutting boards, coasters, floor items, or anything routinely soiled — those designs are reserved for wall pieces."

It is a compliance statement and a differentiator: competitors' adab violations are logged as evidence via /audit.

## Per-order QC checklist
Run for every order before production sign-off (POD: before approving; laser/artisan: before shipping):

- [ ] Design contains ayat or a Name of Allah? → item MUST be a wall piece. If not: stop, substitute a compliant design.
- [ ] Utility item? → text limited to names, duas, geometry.
- [ ] Buyer's custom text reviewed: no Qur'anic verse smuggled into a "custom text" field on a utility item.
- [ ] Arabic/Urdu script verified against the /design-spec sign-off (letterforms, joining, direction, diacritics).
- [ ] Nothing sacred oriented toward the floor in the design's usage position.
- [ ] Packaging/insert: no sacred text on discardable material.

## Edge rulings
- Names of people (even names shared with prophets, e.g. Muhammad, Yusuf) used as personal names on utility items: permitted — they are the person's name, not sacred text. When ambiguous, treat as sacred.
- "Bismillah" on kitchen/dining wall pieces: permitted (wall). On the board/utensil itself: not permitted (soilable).
- Geometric/floral Islamic patterns: permitted anywhere.
- When in doubt: escalate to the operator, default to the stricter reading.
