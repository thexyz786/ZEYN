---
name: review
description: Sunday retro for the Araku sprint — compare plan to actuals, extract a learning, rewrite next week's tasks. Use weekly, or when the founder asks for a retro or review.
---

Read `plan/sprint.md`, `state/tasks.md`, `state/learnings.md`, and all of `data/`.

Produce, in under 350 words:

1. **Numbers vs. plan** — strangers, revenue, spend, and supplier status against this week's targets in `plan/sprint.md`. Deltas only, no adjectives.
2. **The broken assumption** — the single belief this week's data most contradicts. If sales are zero, the broken assumption is about the founder's behavior, not the market — say so plainly.
3. **Learning** — append it to `state/learnings.md`, dated, phrased as a constraint on future plans ("Do not X until Y"), with the evidence row(s) cited.
4. **Next week** — rewrite the coming week's section of `state/tasks.md`: 5–7 tasks maximum, each naming a person, a place, or a deadline, consistent with every accumulated constraint in `state/learnings.md` and with the academic priorities in `CLAUDE.md`.

Stop and ask before proposing anything that changes the gate, the deadline, or the capital rules.
