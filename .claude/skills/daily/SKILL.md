---
name: daily
description: Morning brief for the Araku sprint. Use at the start of each working day, or when the founder asks "what's today", "morning brief", or "plan my day".
---

Read, in order: `plan/sprint.md` (current week only — today falls between 7 Aug and 15 Sep 2026), `state/tasks.md`, `state/learnings.md`, and the last 15 rows of every file in `data/`.

Then write `state/today.md` and print it. Maximum 200 words, exactly this structure:

**Scoreboard** — strangers X/10 · revenue ₹X · spend ₹X against the ₹30,000 cap · supplier remote_ok Y/N/pending · days to gate: N

**Top 3 today** — drawn from the current sprint week in `state/tasks.md`; each MUST name a person to contact, a place to go, or a deadline to hit. From 31 Aug onward, academic deadlines take slots first.

**Blocked** — what is waiting on someone else, and the unblocking action.

**The uncomfortable one** — the single highest-value action the founder is most likely to skip today.

If no action today touches a live buyer, supplier, or hard deadline, open the brief with the line STALLED DAY and one sentence naming what is being avoided. Do not soften the scoreboard. Respect every constraint in `state/learnings.md` and every rule in `CLAUDE.md`.
