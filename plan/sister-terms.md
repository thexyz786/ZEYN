# Operating Terms — Araku Coffee

**Between:** Hatim Zainuddin ("Hatim") and ________________________ ("she" / "her" below — fill in full name)
**Effective:** 8 August 2026 · **First review:** 15 September 2026

---

### 1. What she runs

From today she co-builds. **From 16 September 2026 she runs it.** That covers sourcing and supplier contact, packing and labelling, delivery, every customer message, the Instagram account, and money handling through the business account.

Hatim sells until 15 September and is available for advice after. He does not run day-to-day operations from another hemisphere.

### 2. What she decides alone

**No approval needed:** packaging and label design, Instagram voice and posting, which market or stall to work, price within ₹350–450 per 250 g bag, and any single spend up to **₹5,000**.

**Hatim's approval needed:** any single spend above ₹5,000, changing supplier, pricing outside the band, and anything that commits money the business has not yet earned.

*This clause is the point of the document. She is not executing instructions sent from Los Angeles; she is making the calls that are in front of her.*

### 3. Money

**To 15 September 2026:** 40% of gross profit, accrued and settled at the gate.
**From 16 September 2026:** 70% of gross profit to her, 30% to Hatim until his starting capital is repaid in full. **After repayment: 100% to her,** and Hatim holds no further economic claim.

Gross profit = revenue − (coffee + packaging + ads). Both parties read the same sheet, so only the arithmetic is ever in question.

*The percentage is not payment for her time — during the sprint the sums are small and both of us know it. It exists so the business is hers on paper before it is hers in practice.*

### 4. Stock and brand

Hatim owns the stock and the brand name until his capital is repaid. On repayment both transfer to her in full, including the Instagram account and the customer list.

### 5. Leaving

Either party may exit on **14 days' written notice, for any reason, without explanation.**

- **She exits before capital is repaid:** she returns remaining stock, the Instagram login, supplier contacts, and the customer list. Her accrued share is paid within 30 days. Nothing is owed by her and nothing is held against her.
- **She exits after full transfer:** it is hers to continue, sell, or close. Hatim gets first refusal on the brand name only.
- **Hatim exits:** she may continue under these terms with the capital-repayment clause void.

*November is named on purpose. The month the novelty wears off is the month this document matters. Leaving in November is a permitted outcome, not a failure.*

### 6. If it does not pass the gate

Below ten unrelated buyers on 15 September the venture closes. Remaining stock sells at cost, her accrued share is paid in full regardless of the outcome, and neither party owes the other anything further.
